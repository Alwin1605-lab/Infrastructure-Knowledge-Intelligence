# Phase 12 Research Hypotheses\n\nThis document pre-registers the pilot hypotheses and their outcomes based on the 5-question pilot evaluation.\n\n### H1: RAG-based architectures provide better evidence retrieval than LLM-only.\n**Status**: SUPPORTED\nRAG-based pipelines retrieved context successfully, whereas LLM-only operates without retrieval.\n\n### H2: KG integration improves entity and relationship coverage.\n**Status**: INCONCLUSIVE\nDue to the pilot's 5-question size, entity coverage differences were observed but are not statistically meaningful yet.\n\n### H3: Hybrid retrieval improves retrieval robustness compared with vector-only retrieval.\n**Status**: INCONCLUSIVE\nSample size too small to demonstrate robust differences between Hybrid RAG and RAG-only.\n\n### H4: Agentic orchestration improves answer-point coverage.\n**Status**: INCONCLUSIVE\nFurther queries are needed to prove whether agentic orchestration consistently outperforms Hybrid RAG.\n\n### H5: Additional reasoning components increase latency.\n**Status**: SUPPORTED\nLatency definitively increased as components (KG, Reranking, Agentic Orchestration) were added.\n
